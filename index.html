Privacy Policy — Quiz Engine

Last updated: May 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Quiz Engine ("the App") handles information when you use it.

1. Data We Collect

Quiz Engine does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All quiz progress, results, and preferences are stored only on your device using local storage. No data ever leaves your device.

2. Third-Party Services

Quiz Engine does not use any third-party analytics, advertising networks, crash reporting, or tracking services. The App makes no network requests during normal use.

3. Children's Privacy

Quiz Engine is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the App collects no data, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

4. Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will publish the new version at this URL and update the "Last updated" date above.
